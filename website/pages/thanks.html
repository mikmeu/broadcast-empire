Models

Everything in the office and the town is built from free model kits. All of them are in the public domain (CC0).

Kenney
Furniture Kit, Mini Characters, City Kit Commercial, City Kit Industrial, City Kit Suburban and Train Kit: the furniture, the people, the towns of every region and the train that passes the office.
Quaternius
Animated Man and Animated Woman, who became Uncle Tabby and Penny Cash. Found through Poly Pizza.

Sound and voices

Kenney
UI Audio, Impact Sounds and Music Jingles: every click, every desk put down and the jingle at a milestone. Public domain (CC0).
ElevenLabs
The voices of Uncle Tabby and Penny Cash in the tutorial. They are AI-generated.

Typefaces

Fjalla One
By Sorkin Type, for the headings. SIL Open Font License.
Assistant
By the Assistant Project Authors, after Adobe's Source Sans Pro, for the text. SIL Open Font License.
Fontsource
Packages both, so the game carries its own fonts.
