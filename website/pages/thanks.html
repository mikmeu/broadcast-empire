Models

Everything in the office and the town is built from free model kits. All of them are in the public domain (CC0).

Kenney
Furniture Kit, Mini Characters, City Kit Commercial, City Kit Industrial, City Kit Suburban, City Kit Roads, Car Kit and Train Kit: the furniture, the people, the towns of every region, their streets, the cars in them and the train that passes the office.
Quaternius
Animated Man and Animated Woman, who became Uncle Tabby and Penny Cash, and Man in Suit and Woman in Dress, who became Sonny Sparkle and Lotta Glitter, the hosts of the awards night. Found through Poly Pizza.

Sound and voices

Kenney
UI Audio, Impact Sounds and Music Jingles: every click, every desk put down and the jingle at a milestone. Public domain (CC0).
ElevenLabs
The voices of Uncle Tabby and Penny Cash in the tutorial. They are AI-generated.
