# Requests

What is still wanted for Broadcast Empire now lives on GitHub: one issue per request, on the board **Broadcast Empire** (https://github.com/users/mikmeu/projects/2), with its stage, priority, size and the version it is planned for. How a request goes from a message to a release, and what you say to approve it, is in [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md). What has been released is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); how the game is built and why is in [Decisions.md](Decisions.md).

## Playtest notes

Send them to support@broadcastempire.online, put them in the form on the website, or open an issue: each becomes an issue of its own, sorted and waiting for your `/approve`. It helps to say which build (it is on the home screen and at the bottom of Settings, for example "Alpha 0.16.0"), which system, and what you were doing.

## To watch while playtesting

Not requests yet: things the tests cannot judge, or that were not checked on a real machine.

- **The traffic lights (0.11.0).** A street gets green once someone waits on the other one, after at least 2.4 seconds of its own. Say whether the town looks jammed, or the waits too long. An ordered taxi is now and then a second late at the door.
- **Overtaking (0.11.0)** happens behind a bus at its stop or a taxi at the door, and only when nothing is coming. Say whether you ever see it, and whether it looks right.
- **The milestone pop-up (0.11.0)** stops the game. Early on, milestones come close together (on the air, a month on the air, a first profit, a first sponsor): say whether it gets in the way.

- **Hard difficulty (0.16.0).** The master control room, its technician and the CEO's office cost about €2,100 a month from the start on hard. The test players say a company that stays in one region now often cannot carry that; say whether hard should be eased, or the technician only be needed once there is a second channel.
- **Technicians at the desks (0.16.0).** One technician works the master control room and one TV control room, or two TV control rooms, and has no time left for repairs. Say whether a company with television runs short of technicians too easily, and whether 75% for a studio nobody works is too harsh or too mild.
- **The cable view (0.16.0)** was checked in a browser by script, not by hand. Say whether the cables are easy to follow, and whether you want to lay them yourself after all.
- **Morale and strikes (0.15.0).** A canteen alone now brings morale to about 80; the cook, the post and monthly meetings take it to the 90s. Say whether that is worth what they cost, and whether a strike ever comes as a surprise or comes too easily. The test players never see one; a company without cleaners does, after some months.
- **The color scheme by the time of day (0.15.0)** turns dark at the end of a winter month and stays light in summer. Say whether it turns too often at high speed, or whether summer should have its dark evenings too.
- **Morale standing still at 60 with a complete canteen.** It could not be reproduced. If it happens, note what stood in the canteen.
- **A talk station.** Penny suggests one (0.10.0); the test players never built one, so nobody knows yet whether it pays. Say how it goes.
- **Sponsors at 70%.** A sponsor known for years pays 70% over the ad rate where every sponsor used to pay 10% to 50%. Say whether money gets too easy later on.
- **Sales two months sooner.** Since 0.10.0 Sales opens on 1 February when January pays its way (it used to take a month that closed up after all spending, 1 April for the test players). Sponsor money comes sooner, and the test player leads the radio market after five months in more games than before. Say whether the first year got too easy.
- **A game saved in 0.9.0 with TV studios.** Its game shows and soaps move to studios of their own kind when it is loaded. Checked by tests, not by hand.
- **Pace.** A day is 16 seconds at normal speed since 0.16.0 (it was 4); 1970 to 2030 is about 97 hours of play. The test players reach a first profit within three months and lead the radio market within a year; a person will be slower. Say where it drags or rushes.
- **Difficulty (0.12.0).** It now also sets the starting money and the costs, and what milestones bring (R86). Say whether easy is easy enough, and whether hard is hard enough at 5% higher costs.
- **The desktop app on Windows and Linux.** The installers are built on a Mac and have never been run. Look at the 3D office in particular: how smooth it is, and whether anything is missing or black.
- **A small or touch screen.** Not checked since Phase 12: the workplaces list, the small window of a piece of furniture, drawing a room with a finger.
- **Moving a piece of furniture to another room,** and everything about the long building in Amplify Hills and Aerial Fields, were checked by tests more than by hand.
