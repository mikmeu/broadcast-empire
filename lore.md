# Lore

The game plays in a fictional country, Etheria. Your uncle Tabby Loid, a major newspaper giant, lobbied the government for years to finally enable commercial parties to make radio and tv, and that day is here. Uncle Tabby is your first fiancier, and he even lends you his very witty business consultant Penny Cash (might be too on the nose and might change later). Penny Cash is the tutorial of this game and asks the player if her services are needed. She will lead you trough the first steps of setting up the company. She will also appear the first time you go almost bankrupt. When you go bankrupt, Penny can give you a one-time cash injection from your dear uncle.

## Geography

Etheria lies on the sea, which runs along its whole north coast and cuts in as a long bay on the east side. To the west and south it borders other countries. A river runs from the Amplify Hills south-west into Byte Lake, and another runs down the east of Transponder Valley to the bay.

## Politics

Every region has their own Governor and have their own house of state representatives. The number will vary per region. Nation-wide the country has a President and a Vice-President who choose their own ministers. The country has both a senate (formed by a couple of senators per region) and a directly elected house of representatives.

Regional senate elections are every four years, just as the elections of the house, but they are two years apart. The leader of the biggest party in the house will become the newly elected President, the leader of the second biggest party the newly elected Vice-President. The leader of the biggest party in the Senate will be the Senate Speaker.

The senate consists of 136 seats, made up of 50 by Coaxia, 36 of Transponder, 24 of Skywave, 16 of Aerial and 10 of Amplify.

The house consists of 185 seats. In order for a party to be part of the house the party needs at least 250.000 votes. The remainder of the seats will be divided by the remainder of the parties. Major parties include: Etheria Workers Force (EWF), Liberath'49 (LIB), The Green Society (TGS), Righteous Etheria Brothers (REB) and Free Etheria (FE).

## Regions

There are five regions in Etheria. See the maps in design/reference/look.

### Coaxia

Landlocked in the south-west corner of Etheria is Coaxia, named after the metropole city of Coax City. Its only neighbour inside the country is Amplify Hills, to the north-east. It is a place where both young people an professionals move.

Coaxia is by far the most crowded of all the regions. The main capital of Etheria is Coax City with high skyscrapers and busy streets. A lot of young urban professionals have moved to the city to work high-end jobs. Coaxia City also has the largest and highest rated university of the country: Coaxia City University (CCU). The Faculty of Media is internationally known as having the best media and communication studies of the planet.

Altough being the main capital, the government has their residency in the neighbouring town of Turing. Both the senate (formed of senators from all the different regions), the house of representatives (elected directly by the public) and the Turing House (government building) can be found here.

- Main audiences: teens, students, professionals
- Counties: Zuse, Turing, Bush
- Places: the cities Kate and Barbara, Byte Lake, Buch Wildlife Park
- Landmark: Reality Square: a very busy square in the middle of Coax City with a lot of billboards and tv screens.

### Amplify Hills

In the middle of the country, north-east of Coaxia, you can find Amplify Hills. It is the only region that touches all four others. It is a large land mass, but has the smallest population. Families and seniors like to live here because of the nature. Some seniors have never seen any place outside their own small village. There are still a couple of active mines where workers work.

Being spot in the middle of the country means a lot of people travel every day trough one of the busiest train stations of the country: Nipkow Central. They might be so busy getting their rush hour train that they probably would not see the true buty of Nipkow Central, as it is the oldest train stations of Etheria. From there you can also take one of the most beautiful train rides in the world: taking the train to Transponder Valley will lead you straight into the Amplify Hills.

- Audiences: families, seniors, workers
- Counties: Nipkow, Shoenberg, Korn, Ardenne, Hell
- Places: the cities Stroedel and Apfel, the Amplify Hills themselves
- Landmark: Nipkow Central Station: the oldest train station in the country.

### Transponder Valley

In the north-east, with the sea on two sides, you can find Transponder Valley. Thousands of people work in the factories here. It is known in the Union as the place that makes everything: from the apps on your phone, to the chips in your phone, to the phone itself. On the east side of Transponder Valley you can find massive factories that build basically everything, on the west side (near the Amplify Hills and the Meucci Desert) you can find Transponder Techinal University the place where you can find students and professionals working on tomorrow's answers.

- Audiences: workers, professionals, students
- Counties: Meucci, Reis, Bell, Gray, Puskas, Edison
- Places: the cities Bell, Tone and Ring, the Meucci Desert (shared with Skywave Beach)
- Landmark: Transponder Technical Unversity: a big campus next to a big hill.

### Skywave Beach

In the north-west, along the north coast, you can find the busy Skywave Beach. It is where seniors come to relax, families live in large cities and professionals work in large offices. There are also large hotel chains and possible sharks.

The most interesting place in all of Skywave Beach is the Mer Doché Hotel. It was the first of many hotels founded by the newspaper magnate Meridiane Doché, and by far the most eccentric. Only the biggest of stars stay here. It is made of large stones that look like they come straight from the Ancienct Greece.

- Audiences: professionals, families, seniors
- Districts: Hughes, Maxwell, Hertz, Trouton
- Places: the cities Maxwell, Yaht and Bling, Maxwell International Harbor
- Landmark: Mer Doché Hotel: a very extravagant hotel next to the beach, looks almost out of a movie.

### Aerial Fields

In the south-east, on the bay, you can find the rural area. It is the smallest region on the map. What lacks in people is made up of cows, chickens, sheep, alpacas, you name it, they have it. Aerial Fields are the backbone of all things food related in Etheria and its surrounding countries. It is best known for a special herb called Aeia, that has grown in Etheria since before people moved in. Its special workings make everything you cook taste better, and it has not been banned by any world food agency.. yet.

- Audiences: teens, workers, families
- Counties: Lee, Cerf, Kahn, Taylor
- Places: the cities Google, Buzz, Newcast and Bigdata, Newcast International Airport
- Landmark: a statue of a very happy man holding crops of Aeia in the middle of a village roundabout.

## Tabby Loid
Tabby Loid is the CEO and sole shareholder of Loid Enterprises and Loid Capital. Born in 1932 as the child of Anton and Debbie Loid in the hills of Transponder Valley. His father, Anton Loid, was the creator of the very succesful Transponder Gazette, which started as a regional newspaper but grew as the biggest newspaper in the whole of Etheria. In 1943, with the merger of the second largest newspaper in the country The Coax Courant, the company and the paper went further as Etheria Daily, that still exist to-date. Tabby started young in the new company as a paper boy, but worked his way up to a manager by the age of 21.

Just a couple years later, with the purchase of several other newspapers and magazines and as part of the new company Loid Enterprises, young Tabby became the head of the newspaper division. 

After the untimely death of his father, Tabby Loid became the CEO and sole shareholder of Loid Enterprises in 1956. With the profits of the company, Tabby started Loid Capital in 1961, that invested in small media companies. One of which was a radio station broadcasting from the seas by the name of SEA-AM. Commercial broadcasting was still illegal, so the loophole was to broadcast in international waters. But it was still taken off the air only a couple of months later when the government incited a new rule that forbids the broadcasting of commercial stations in the airwaves of Etheria.

After the debacle of SEA-AM, Tabby Loid has been the prominent figure that fought against the law forbidding commercial radio and tv. Most notably, an emotial speech that he gave in September 1969 in both the Senate and the House, after which 67% of the votes were in favour of lifting the ban. On January 1st, 1970 first commercial licenses were awarded.

## Transport

### Railway

The first ever rail connection was in 1839 between Coax City and Turing, the Coax City-Turing Transportation Foundation (CCTF), originally a non-profit foundation. Two years later, the line expanded to Barbara in the South and Apfel in the North.

The second rail connection was between Maxwell and Bling in 1842 I-Max, short for Interrail Maxwell Corporation. It was followed by the Yaht-Bling Line (YBL) a year later. CCTF expanded to Nipkow, with I-Max following suit. Meanwhile in the east, the Bell-Tone-Ring-Line (BTRL) was formed in 1844. I-Max bought YBL in 1845, and expanded the Yaht-Bling line to Stroedel and Nipkow, offering two lines from Skywave Beach to Amplify Hills.

Nipkow quickly became a central hub in Etheria, so with the expansion of the train station in 1852, two new transportation companies were formed: Nipkow North Railway (NNR) trough the Amplify Hills ending in Bell and Nipkow-Newcast Co. (NipNewCo) going trough Goggle and Newcast, later connecting with Buzz and Ring. By 1855 all of Etheria was connected by rail.

The years ahead were a bit rough for the railway companies: higher maintance fee than expected and locomotives that were not equipped for certain terrain types (mountains, desert). NNR filed for bankrupcy in 1860. In order to not completely cut off Transponder Valley from the rest of the country, the government stepped in and build a railway connection between Bell and Maxwell (National Etherian Railway Association - NERA). It was not a popular one, people missed the mountain train that was the Nipkow-Bell-connection. Also, travel times were doubled. By 1861, the government deciced to pick up the defuct Nipkow-Bell line. Ticket prices were higher than before, but the reset of the railway made it one of the most popular in the country.

But the commercial railway companies were not all in the black. Both BTRL and NipNewCo were hanging on by a thread in 1864. CCTF filed a law suit at the end of 1864 claiming that the Etherian government were "creating unfair competition in the railway market" by allowing the NERA to exist. The law suit was lost by CCTF, losing them a lot of money. I-Max stepped in and bought the company in 1865, merging their train lines. A new train system started: fast-riding trains skipping over smaller stations: from Maxwell to Nipkow and from Nipkow to Coax City. A less often-running service does run between al the smaller towns. Prioritizing bigger cities had its profit, I-Max became the highest valued company in the country. It takes over BTRL in 1867, with only the troubling NipNewCo and the government owned NERA as its direct competitor. By then, I-Max had over 85% market share.

Afraid that the I-Max company became too powerful, the Etherian government enacted a law in 1869 banning any company in the country to have more than 50% market share of any market (monopoly). By 1870 I-Max had to split in two smaller companies: MaxNorth and MaxSouth. To stop any unfair competition the NERA company put up the fleet and the line for sale, it was bought my MaxNorth. The NERA company is from that point the official maintainer of any station or railway in the country. 

New management decides in 1901 to rename the NipNewCo-company to the Etherian Eagle, because the eagle was the favorite animal of the newly elected CEO. A couple of years follow with mismanagement, including a complete new fleet Diesel trains from south of the Union that malfunctioned from the start. Etherian Eagle has to file for bankrupcy in January of 1902, but there is no-one to take over their lines, causing a major connection problem in the country. Both MaxNorth and MaxSouth cannot start their service there, as their market share would cross the monopoly-line. A quick proposition by the government allows the NERA to exploit the lines temporarily with rental trains of both MaxNorth and MaxSouth. It was a temporary solution, so the government quickly seek out a solution.

The solution came in a spin-off of the MaxSouth company: Etheria Eeastern (which was a nod to the old Etherian Eagle company). It started operating in 1903 with a new fleet of trains.

The decades after a few rebrandings occur. Both MaxSouth and MaxNorth decide in 1920 to continue with different names, suggesting to the customer they were different companies. MaxNorth becomes Skyponder Railways (Skywave and Transponder combined) and MaxSouth becomes Southern Etherian Railway Company (SERC). To make it easier for customers, Skyponder and SERC bring back the Maxwell-Nipkow-Coax line (called CityDirect). Skyponder renames it's company in NORTHERN in 1931, and changes it back in Skyponder in 1942.

In the 50s questions starting to rise whether the rail network should be nationalized. Many customers are dissatisfied with the customer services of most railways, claiming that because they have no direct competition in their regions, they don't have to compete. By the end of the 50s all three railway companies have all combined services and are sharing things like fleet, engineers, even schedulers. 

That the system was not working became even more clear in the 60s when there were multiple law suits against malpractices in the three companies. There were systems set in place to enrich a wealthy few, built over decades of corruption. The government did not want to interrupt, as they were affraid it would cause problems to the service. By 1969, the NERA started a department that was put in place to check officials in railway companies. But even the NERA itself was riddled with corruption, as was revealed by an independant research in 1970.

What followed were a couple of riots, strikes by personnel and eventually a landslide victory for a socialist government. In an evict in 1972 the NERA was cleaned up and renamed to the Etherian Transportation Foundation. All transportation companies (busses and trains included) will have to be dissolved by 1974, all services will from then on be serviced by the new transportation foundation.

Etherian Eastern was dissolved as the first company, being the smallest. Immediately problems started to arise: the ETF did not have the right personnel to pick up the pieces. The dissolvement of Skyponder and SERC were postponed untill further notice. However, with a very uncertain future, both companies saw a lot of people quit their jobs. It was not a great year to take the train. By 1977, ETF was running all services. To avoid any further corruption the maintenance of the trains and stations were put into a new foundation: the Etherian Transpor Maintenance Foundation (ETMF). Commercial spaces in train stations were sold to either the SERC and Skyponder, who were at that point only doing commercial bus services. SERC and Skyponder merged in 1979 into TransEtheria, with subdivisions TransEtheria Coaches and TransEtheria Commercial Retail.

### EPB: Etherian Public Broadcast
The government founded the Etherian Public Broadcast foundation in 1925 to service the first radio broadcasts in Turing. It programmed two hours a day for the latest news announcements and played so now and then classical music. Commerical radio stations weren't allowed from the start, as the government wanted the new media to be heavily regulated. Wrong information might result into a riot. The country just came out of one of the heaviest crashes of the market in the history of crashes.

The station was called "Turing Radio Set" by the people, but it hadn't had an offical name until 1927 when hosts started to use the name in their own broadcasts. As it turned out, the broadcasts could not reach Transponder Valley that well over the Amplify hills. It was then when the government opened a small department in Bell with the name "Bell Radio". It turned out to be too expensive to run, so the radio station started broadcasting recorded messages from Turing. It renamed the broadcasts to "The Etherian Public Broadcast", removing the local town names from the name tag.

In 1935 the government started a second channel, renaming both channels to Radio 1 and Radio 2. Radio 1 started to focus on talk-only (news and sports) and Radio 2 on music-only.

In 1951 EPB started with the first TV experiments. The channel was simply called "EPB-TV", the name it still carries today. The "EPB"-name tag was also added to the radio shortly thereafter. 

By 1970 the government allowed commercial channels, giving the public broadcaster their first rivals.
