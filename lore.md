# Lore

The game plays in a fictional country, Etheria. Your uncle Tabby Loid, a major newspaper giant, lobbied the government for years to finally enable commercial parties to make radio and tv, and that day is here. Uncle Tabby is your first fiancier, and he even lends you his very witty business consultant Penny Cash (might be too on the nose and might change later). Penny Cash is the tutorial of this game and asks the player if her services are needed. She will lead you trough the first steps of setting up the company. She will also appear the first time you go almost bankrupt. When you go bankrupt, Penny can give you a one-time cash injection from your dear uncle.

## Geography

Etheria lies on the sea, which runs along its whole north coast and cuts in as a long bay on the east side. To the west and south it borders other countries. A river runs from the Amplify Hills south-west into Byte Lake, and another runs down the east of Transponder Valley to the bay.

## Politics

Every region has their own Governor and have their own house of state representatives. The number will vary per region. Nation-wide the country has a President and a Vice-President who choose their own ministers. The country has both a senate (formed by a couple of senators per region) and a directly elected house of representatives.

Regional senate elections are every four years, just as the elections of the house, but they are two years apart. The leader of the biggest party in the house will become the newly elected President, the leader of the second biggest party the newly elected Vice-President. The leader of the biggest party in the Senate will be the Senate Speaker.

The senate consists of 136 seats, made up of 50 by Coaxia, 36 of Transponder, 24 of Skywave, 16 of Aerial and 10 of Amplify.

The house consists of 185 seats. In order for a party to be part of the house the party needs at least 250.000 votes. The remainder of the seats will be divided by the remainder of the parties. Major parties include: Etheria Workers Force (EWF), Liberath'49 (LIB), The Green Society (TGS), Righteous Etheria Brothers (REB) and Free Etheria (FE).

## Regions

There are five regions in Etheria. See the maps in design/reference/look.

### Coaxia

Landlocked in the south-west corner of Etheria is Coaxia, named after the metropole city of Coax City. Its only neighbour inside the country is Amplify Hills, to the north-east. It is a place where both young people an professionals move.

Coaxia is by far the most crowded of all the regions. The main capital of Etheria is Coax City with high skyscrapers and busy streets. A lot of young urban professionals have moved to the city to work high-end jobs. Coaxia City also has the largest and highest rated university of the country: Coaxia City University (CCU). The Faculty of Media is internationally known as having the best media and communication studies of the planet.

Altough being the main capital, the government has their residency in the neighbouring town of Turing. Both the senate (formed of senators from all the different regions), the house of representatives (elected directly by the public) and the Turing House (government building) can be found here.

- Main audiences: teens, students, professionals
- Counties: Zuse, Turing, Bush
- Places: the cities Kate and Barbara, Byte Lake, Buch Wildlife Park
- Landmark: Reality Square: a very busy square in the middle of Coax City with a lot of billboards and tv screens.

### Amplify Hills

In the middle of the country, north-east of Coaxia, you can find Amplify Hills. It is the only region that touches all four others. It is a large land mass, but has the smallest population. Families and seniors like to live here because of the nature. Some seniors have never seen any place outside their own small village. There are still a couple of active mines where workers work.

Being spot in the middle of the country means a lot of people travel every day trough one of the busiest train stations of the country: Nipkow Central. They might be so busy getting their rush hour train that they probably would not see the true buty of Nipkow Central, as it is the oldest train stations of Etheria. From there you can also take one of the most beautiful train rides in the world: taking the train to Transponder Valley will lead you straight into the Amplify Hills.

- Audiences: families, seniors, workers
- Counties: Nipkow, Shoenberg, Korn, Ardenne, Hell
- Places: the cities Stroedel and Apfel, the Amplify Hills themselves
- Landmark: Nipkow Central Station: the oldest train station in the country.

### Transponder Valley

In the north-east, with the sea on two sides, you can find Transponder Valley. Thousands of people work in the factories here. It is known in the Union as the place that makes everything: from the apps on your phone, to the chips in your phone, to the phone itself. On the east side of Transponder Valley you can find massive factories that build basically everything, on the west side (near the Amplify Hills and the Meucci Desert) you can find Transponder Techinal University the place where you can find students and professionals working on tomorrow's answers.

- Audiences: workers, professionals, students
- Counties: Meucci, Reis, Bell, Gray, Puskas, Edison
- Places: the cities Bell, Tone and Ring, the Meucci Desert (shared with Skywave Beach)
- Landmark: Transponder Technical Unversity: a big campus next to a big hill.

### Skywave Beach

In the north-west, along the north coast, you can find the busy Skywave Beach. It is where seniors come to relax, families live in large cities and professionals work in large offices. There are also large hotel chains and possible sharks.

The most interesting place in all of Skywave Beach is the Mer Doché Hotel. It was the first of many hotels founded by the newspaper magnate Meridiane Doché, and by far the most eccentric. Only the biggest of stars stay here. It is made of large stones that look like they come straight from the Ancienct Greece.

- Audiences: professionals, families, seniors
- Districts: Hughes, Maxwell, Hertz, Trouton
- Places: the cities Maxwell, Yaht and Bling, Maxwell International Harbor
- Landmark: Mer Doché Hotel: a very extravagant hotel next to the beach, looks almost out of a movie.

### Aerial Fields

In the south-east, on the bay, you can find the rural area. It is the smallest region on the map. What lacks in people is made up of cows, chickens, sheep, alpacas, you name it, they have it. Aerial Fields are the backbone of all things food related in Etheria and its surrounding countries. It is best known for a special herb called Aeia, that has grown in Etheria since before people moved in. Its special workings make everything you cook taste better, and it has not been banned by any world food agency.. yet.

- Audiences: teens, workers, families
- Counties: Lee, Cerf, Kahn, Taylor
- Places: the cities Google, Buzz, Newcast and Bigdata, Newcast International Airport
- Landmark: a statue of a very happy man holding crops of Aeia in the middle of a village roundabout.

## Tabby Loid
Tabby Loid is the CEO and sole shareholder of Loid Enterprises and Loid Capital. Born in 1932 as the child of Anton and Debbie Loid in the hills of Transponder Valley. His father, Anton Loid, was the creator of the very succesful Transponder Gazette, which started as a regional newspaper but grew as the biggest newspaper in the whole of Etheria. In 1943, with the merger of the second largest newspaper in the country The Coax Courant, the company and the paper went further as Etheria Daily, that still exist to-date. Tabby started young in the new company as a paper boy, but worked his way up to a manager by the age of 21.

Just a couple years later, with the purchase of several other newspapers and magazines and as part of the new company Loid Enterprises, young Tabby became the head of the newspaper division. 

After the timely death of his father, Tabby Loid became the CEO and sole shareholder of Loid Enterprises in 1956. With the profits of the company, Tabby started Loid Capital in 1961, that invested in small media companies. One of which was a radio station broadcasting from the seas by the name of SEA-AM. Commercial broadcasting was still illegal, so the loophole was to broadcast in international waters. But it was still taken off the air only a couple of months later when the government incited a new rule that forbids the broadcasting of commercial stations in the airwaves of Etheria.

After the debacle of SE-AM, Tabby Loid has been the prominent figure that fought against the law forbidding commercial radio and tv. Most notably, an emotial speech that he gave in September 1969 in both the Senate and the House, after which 67% of the votes were in favour of banning the law. On January 1st, 1970 first commercial licenses were awarded.
